Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 6, 2026

Kuatowise has no user accounts, no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking SDKs. Your saved cards, streak, and settings live on your device. The only thing that leaves your device is the minimum needed to fetch the day's card and verify a subscription — and none of it identifies you personally.

Information stored on your device

The following stays on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Cards you save
  • Your reading streak
  • Your daily reminder time and notification preference
  • Whether you have completed onboarding
  • Your subscription status (whether Pro is active, and when it expires)

This data is stored using the operating system's standard local storage. Deleting the app removes it.

Information sent to our server

To show you content, the app contacts our backend server. A request may include:

  • Your app language (for example "en" or "ja"), so we return the card in the right language
  • Your timezone offset, so "today's card" matches your local day
  • The numeric IDs of cards you are viewing, so we can return their text

We do not ask for, receive, or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, or any advertising identifier. Requests are not tied to an account or a persistent profile. Standard server logs may briefly record an IP address for security and reliability; these are not used to build a profile.

Subscriptions

Kuatowise Pro is an optional subscription sold through the Apple App Store. Apple processes all payments — we never see your card number or billing details. When you subscribe or restore a purchase, the app sends Apple's purchase receipt to our server so we can confirm the subscription is valid. The receipt contains purchase information but no personal identity.

Notifications

The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. It does not use push servers and does not send any data off your device. You can change the time or turn it off in Settings, or in the iOS Settings app.

Children

Kuatowise is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Because Kuatowise does not maintain accounts or personal profiles, there is no personal data set tied to you for us to export or delete. Removing the app deletes all on-device data. If you have questions about your rights under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or CCPA, contact us at the address below.

Third parties

Apple provides the App Store and processes subscription payments under Apple's own privacy policy. That is the complete list. Kuatowise does not integrate advertising networks or analytics providers.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and post the revised version at this URL.
